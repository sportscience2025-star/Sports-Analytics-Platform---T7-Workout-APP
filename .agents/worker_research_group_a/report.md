## 3. Methodological Rationale & Impact on Training Programming

### 3.1 Atletica Leggera
The 14 new sources dramatically enrich the system's athletic rationale engine:
- **Resisted Sprinting & Heavy Sleds**: Morin (2017) and Cahill (2020) provide evidence-based parameters for using sled loads up to 80% body mass to overload horizontal force ($F_0$) during early acceleration without technique degradation.
- **Sprint Biomechanics & Curvilinear Running**: Churchill (2015) documents the asymmetrical hip/ankle stabilization requirements during bend sprinting; Bezodis (2015) and Nagahara (2014) provide precise kinematic determinants for block starts and spatiotemporal transitions.
- **Hurdles & Jumps**: Hanley (2021) and Rowley (2021) establish flight parabolic mechanics and 7 vs 8 step approach strategies; Linthorne (2005) and Allen (2016) ground long and triple jump takeoff velocity trade-offs.
- **Reactive Strength Index (RSI)**: Jarvis et al. (2022) meta-analysis correlates fast stretch-shortening cycle (SSC) metrics (<250 ms) to sprinting performance.

### 3.2 Sollevamento Pesi
- **NSCA Official Position Stand (2023)**: Comfort, Haff, Suchomel, Soriano et al. provide the international benchmark for weightlifting transfer to athletic sprinting, jumping, and RFD.
- **Pulling Derivatives**: Suchomel (2015, 2017, 2020) and Soriano (2019) establish that removing the catch phase (e.g. jump shrug, clean pull) yields higher peak velocity, power, and RFD with reduced joint stress.
- **Cluster Set Structures**: Tufano, Brown & Haff (2017) validate intra-set rest intervals (10-30s) to sustain mechanical power and velocity across high-load strength sessions.
- **Optimal Power Loading**: Soriano et al. (2015) meta-analysis proves weightlifting movements achieve peak power at higher relative loads (70-85% 1RM) compared to traditional squats/jumps (30-60% 1RM).

### 3.3 Velocity-Based Training (VBT)
- **Velocity Loss Thresholds (VLT)**: Pareja-Blanco et al. (2017, 2020) demonstrate that 10-20% velocity loss produces equivalent strength gains to 40% loss, but preserves Type IIX fast twitch fibers and triples jump height improvements.
- **Two-Point Method**: Garcia-Ramos & Jaric (2023) and Perez-Castilla (2021) validate the fatigue-free 2-load assessment protocol for daily 1RM estimation.
- **Real-Time Biofeedback**: Weakley et al. (2019) proves that instant velocity display attenuates velocity loss and increases athlete motivation.
- **Mobile & Wearable Validation**: Balsalobre-Fernandez (2016, 2018) validates accessible field tools (PowerLift app, PUSH band) against gold-standard linear position transducers.
