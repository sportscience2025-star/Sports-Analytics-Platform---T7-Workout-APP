## Executive Summary

An exhaustive empirical audit was conducted on the deliverables of **Task 1** (Scientific Database Expansion and Modularization):
1. **Source Authenticity & PMID Verification**: Every single PubMed URL and PMID across all 260 scientific sources in the 7 thematic files (`atletica.js`, `sollevamento-pesi.js`, `fisiologia.js`, `vbt.js`, `biomeccanica.js`, `clinico.js`, `sport-squadra.js`) was cross-referenced programmatically against the official **NCBI PubMed Entrez E-Utilities API** (`https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esummary.fcgi`).
   - **Result**: **FAIL** — Over 53% of all PMIDs in the database are corrupted, hallucinated, or completely mismatched:
     - **136 PMIDs** return completely different papers on PubMed (e.g. oncology, gynecology, astrophysics, animal biology, tobacco price policies) instead of the sports science studies claimed.
     - **2 PMIDs** (`20024937` in `vbt.js` and `26672828` in `sport-squadra.js`) do **not exist on PubMed at all** (NCBI error: `"cannot get document summary"`).
     - **5 PMIDs** newly generated by `worker_research_group_a` during Task 1 contain hallucinated PMIDs pointing to chronic lymphocytic leukemia (`sci_sprint_nagahara_2014`), dietary beta-carotene bioavailability (`sci_power_soriano_2015`), 4D ultrafast electron microscopy (`sci_weightlifting_hackett_2016`), kettlebell vs weightlifting (`sci_weightlifting_arabatzi_2012_coactivation`), and voice gender perception (`sci_vbt_balsalobre_2018_app`).
     - **131 PMIDs** carried over from the legacy 161-entry baseline in `knowledge-base.js` possess mismatched/corrupted PMIDs.
2. **Aggregator Architecture (`js/data/sources/index.js`)**: **PASS** — Contains zero hardcoded study objects. Genuinely aggregates the 7 area arrays from `window.App.Sources`, performs Set-based ID deduplication, provides filtering methods (`getScientificSourcesByArea`), and dynamically loads files in Node.js test environments.
3. **Backward-Compatibility Facade (`js/knowledge-base.js`)**: **PASS** — Reduced from 3,407 lines to 44 lines. Genuinely delegates to `js/data/sources/index.js` without any duplicate arrays or facade mocks.
4. **Test Suite Non-Weakening (`test_check.py`)**: **PASS** — Was not weakened. Cache assertion updated to `t7-workout-v77`. Delimiter checks extended to all 8 modular source files and `knowledge-base.js`.

Under the governing rule:

1. Run an automated NCBI lookup script to retrieve the genuine PMIDs for all 136 mismatched studies based on exact title and first author searches.

3. Replace the 2 non-existent PMIDs (`20024937` -> `20180176`, `26672828` -> `26675089`).
4. Re-run NCBI verification to ensure 100% genuine PMID matching before approving Task 1.
